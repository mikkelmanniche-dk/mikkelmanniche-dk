## Building now

**[dk-regnskab-mcp](https://github.com/mikkelmanniche-dk/dk-regnskab-mcp)** gives Claude the published annual reports of Danish companies. It reads the XBRL filings at the Danish Business Authority and returns key figures, handling the parts that trip up naive parsers: dimensional facts, conflicting values, legally omitted revenue, two taxonomies.
